Jotter is a notes app that keeps your notes on your phone and lets you choose where they sync. It needs no account, has no ads and no tracking, and works without a network.

<b>Notes</b>
<ul>
<li>Markdown notes with a preview, including task lists with checkboxes you can tap</li>
<li>Tags, pinning, archive and trash</li>
<li>Full-text search across all notes</li>
<li>"Share to Jotter" saves text from any app as a new note</li>
</ul>

<b>Sync on your terms</b>
<ul>
<li>Every device keeps its own copy of all notes in a local database, so the app works offline</li>
<li>Sync through a WebDAV server you choose, such as Nextcloud, a NAS or a mail provider's storage</li>
<li>Or sync with the Jotter desktop app on your own PC over Tailscale: the phone sends its pairing token only while Tailscale's VPN carries the app</li>
<li>Edits made on two devices at once are merged; where they clash, Jotter shows both and lets you choose</li>
<li>Sync runs in the background about every 15 minutes, and when you leave the app</li>
</ul>

<b>Privacy</b>
<ul>
<li>Passwords and tokens are encrypted with a key kept in the Android Keystore, and are never in a backup</li>
<li>Android's backup copies the notes only where the backup is end-to-end encrypted</li>
<li>Jotter contacts no server except the ones you add</li>
</ul>
